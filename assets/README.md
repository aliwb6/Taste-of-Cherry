# Film photographs: source and rights record

Downloaded 24 October 2026 from the official **Janus Films** page for *Taste of Cherry*:

- [Film page](https://www.janusfilms.com/films/1037), under **Downloads → Stills**.
- [Original promotional ZIP](https://s3.amazonaws.com/criterion-production/janus_promo_packages/338-/Taste%20of%20Cherry%20Stills_original.zip).

All three photographs are authentic distributor-supplied stills from *Taste of Cherry* (Abbas Kiarostami, 1997). Cinematography: Homayoun Payvar. Courtesy of Janus Films. Copyright belongs to the film's respective rights holders, not this project. No individual still photographer is named in the package.

## Original file record (before manual image selections)

The user later selected additional images in `index.html` and replaced some filenames below. This list records the original distributor download, not the current slide mapping. The sizing fix preserves every current image file and selection.

- `stills/soldier.jpg`: original `45 image 2.jpg`, 3191 × 2057 pixels. The young soldier sits in Mr. Badii's car; dry hills appear through the window. Used on slides 2, 4, and 5.
- `stills/bagheri.jpg`: original `45 image 3.jpg`, 3139 × 2037 pixels. Mr. Bagheri talks in the car. Used on slides 6 and 8.
- `stills/gates.jpg`: original `45 image 4.jpg`, 3107 × 2105 pixels. Mr. Badii walks through open gates. Used on slides 1, 3, 7, and 9. On slide 3 this is an example of the director's work, **not a portrait of Kiarostami**.

The sizing fix does not alter image files. Photographs scale to their allocated slide width using `width: 100%` and `height: auto`. Each figure follows the photograph’s natural aspect ratio without padding, background panels, or fixed heights. The complete image is shown without stretching or cropping. Text sits outside the photographs.

## Permission limits and missing images

Janus publicly offers this package as film promotional material. Neither the film page nor the ZIP supplies an explicit licence for unrestricted reuse, public website hosting, or repository redistribution. **A downloadable press package is not a general image licence.** These local copies are prepared for the requested classroom film discussion; no claim is made that public redistribution has been cleared. Check the classroom's permitted use with the relevant institution/rightsholder before showing them. Request permission from Janus via the contact details on its film page before public hosting or distributing this asset folder. Attribution alone does not grant permission.

No high-resolution road-only landscape or high-resolution close-up of Mr. Badii in his car was available in this package. `45 image 1.jpg` is a 350 × 180 close-up of Badii; it was deliberately excluded because it is unsuitable for projection. The deck therefore reuses three verified high-resolution photographs instead of inventing nine different scenes or substituting unrelated pictures. The missing road-only photograph remains an explicitly documented limitation.

The local commit may include these classroom working assets, but it must not be pushed to a public repository or deployed until public reproduction rights are confirmed.
